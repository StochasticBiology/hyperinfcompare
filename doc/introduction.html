Compare inferences from hypercubic EvAM models

Iain Johnston

Introduction

Perform statistical comparisons of the size and reproducibility of differences between evolutionary accumulation models (EvAMs) learned from the hyperinf ecosystem.

Associated with the manuscript "A method for comparing inferred evolutionary accumulation dynamics across covariates and model structures".
